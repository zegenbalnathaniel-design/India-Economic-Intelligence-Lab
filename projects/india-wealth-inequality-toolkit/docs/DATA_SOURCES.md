## Where to get real Indian income/wealth distribution data (not bundled)

This toolkit does not ship real microdata. If you want to run it against real data, these are defensible starting points:

| Source | Provider | What it has | URL pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| World Inequality Database | World Inequality Lab | Income/wealth share series by country, including India, back to the early 20th century | worldinequality.org |
| PLFS / consumption surveys | MoSPI (Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation) | Household consumption and employment microdata | mospi.gov.in |
| PovcalNet / World Bank microdata library | World Bank | Cross-country income/consumption distributions | worldbank.org |
| All-India Debt and Investment Survey (AIDIS) | NSSO | Household assets and liabilities (closest to a wealth survey) | mospi.gov.in |

Always record, for any dataset you bring in: **source, provider, URL, definition, unit, frequency, date coverage, transformations applied, and known limitations** — the convention used throughout this project's documentation.

## Limitations of available data generally

- Household surveys systematically undersample and undercount the very wealthy (top-tail underreporting is well documented in the inequality-measurement literature).
- Income and wealth are conceptually and empirically distinct; a dataset measuring one should not be relabeled as the other.
- Cross-survey comparisons (e.g. NSSO rounds over time) are complicated by changing survey methodology and recall periods.
