```markdown
### Economics × Data × Technology

I build open-source computational projects exploring economics, finance, education, and Indian economic data — translating economic theory into code you can actually run, change the assumptions of, and see break under different ones.

Each project below is independent: its own repo, its own README, its own tests. They share a common approach — keep theory, data, model assumptions, simulation, and empirical result clearly distinct, and say explicitly when something is illustrative rather than real.

**Fiscal & monetary policy**
- [`india-economic-policy-simulator`](https://github.com/<username>/india-economic-policy-simulator) — a small open-economy AD/AS model (fiscal multiplier, Phillips curve, debt dynamics, crowding out) plus an empirical bank-performance/repo-rate analysis.

**Wealth, inequality & housing**
- [`india-wealth-inequality-toolkit`](https://github.com/<username>/india-wealth-inequality-toolkit) — Gini, Lorenz curves, percentile shares, the Palma ratio, and a household wealth-composition simulator.
- [`india-housing-affordability`](https://github.com/<username>/india-housing-affordability) — price-to-income, EMI, and loan-to-value metrics across major Indian cities.

**Investing & portfolio theory**
- [`portfolio-optimization-lab`](https://github.com/<username>/portfolio-optimization-lab) — Modern Portfolio Theory derived from scratch via Lagrange multipliers (minimum-variance and tangency portfolios, the efficient frontier), over a configurable global multi-exchange stock universe.
- [`sip-monte-carlo`](https://github.com/<username>/sip-monte-carlo) — a Monte Carlo simulator for systematic-investment-plan outcomes: percentile ranges, not single-number guesses.

**Prices & data**
- [`personal-inflation-index`](https://github.com/<username>/personal-inflation-index) — build your own consumption basket and see how your inflation can differ from the official CPI.
- [`india-economic-data-observatory`](https://github.com/<username>/india-economic-data-observatory) — a standardized ingestion/validation pipeline for Indian economic indicators.
- [`india-econ`](https://github.com/<username>/india-econ) — a small, honestly-scoped, pip-installable Python package for pulling a handful of Indian economic indicators.

**Education**
- [`economics-interactive-lab`](https://github.com/<username>/economics-interactive-lab) — an extensible platform of interactive micro/macro/finance modules, each with its own equation, controls, and worked experiment.

---

None of this is investment advice or a policy recommendation — it's an attempt to make economic reasoning something you can poke at, not just read about.
```
